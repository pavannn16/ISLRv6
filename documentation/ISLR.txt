Dataset
Summary
The Isolated Sign Language Recognition corpus (version 1.0) details:
Hand and facial landmarks from Mediapipe on ~100k videos of isolated signs.
21 Deaf signers participated with a 250-sign vocabulary.
Language: American Sign Language.
Dataset Structure:
Data Instances: Example data field with 'frame', 'type', 'landmark_index', 'x', 'y', 'z'.
Data Fields: Detailed data field information available on Kaggle.

U. & P.U. Patel Department of Computer Engineering

Dataset Creation and Curation
Curation Rationale:
Dataset represents 250 first concepts taught to infants in any language.
Aim to create isolated sign recognizer for educational games to help hearing
parents learn ASL.
Importance of avoiding Language Deprivation Syndrome in Deaf children.
Source Data:
21 signers recruited by the Deaf Professional Arts Network.
Randomly selected English concepts to sign with smartphones.

Data Collection and
Normalization
Data Collection Process:
Signers recorded videos while signing each concept.
Video extraction with buffer 0.5 seconds before button press and after button
release.
Variants of signs due to background, region, fingerspelling, and more.
Data Normalisation:
Handling left and right-handed signers, switching signing hands.
Recognition system to handle variations.

App

Overview

App Implementation
The Isolated Sign Language Recognition app allows users to capture sign
language gestures and obtain real-time predictions.
The app uses the Mediapipe library to extract hand and facial landmarks from the
user's video feed.
The captured landmarks are processed and used as input to the trained
TensorFlow Lite model for prediction.
The predicted sign is then displayed to the user along with its confidence score.

U. & P.U. Patel Department of Computer Engineering

App Architecture
The app is built using the Streamlit framework, which allows for easy integration
of machine learning models into web applications.
Key Components:
Video Capture: The app uses the user's webcam to capture real-time video.
Landmark Extraction: Mediapipe is used to extract hand and facial landmarks
from each video frame.
Model Inference: The landmarks are fed into the TensorFlow Lite model to obtain
predictions.
Display: The predicted sign and its confidence score are displayed to the user.

U. & P.U. Patel Department of Computer Engineering

App Demonstration
The app is simple to use:
The user sets the duration (in seconds) for capturing the sign gesture.
After clicking the "Start Capture" button, the webcam captures video for the
specified duration.
The landmarks are extracted using Mediapipe, and the video is processed frame
by frame.
Once the video capture is complete, the captured landmarks are passed to the
TensorFlow Lite model for prediction.
The predicted sign and its confidence score are displayed to the user
